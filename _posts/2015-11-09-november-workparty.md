November Workparty of 34 volunteers was an epic effort and result from many groups:

- JSG of course
- 20 8-11 year olds from Nature Kids;

![DSCN0356]({{ '/assets/website/2015/11/DSCN0356-300x225.jpg' | relative_url }}){: style="max-width: 100%; height: auto" loading="lazy"}

- @EvergreenBC who brought the tools and cinamon buns.
- Parks Board removed blackberry & knotweed then supplied us 140 native trees, shrubs   & ferns;
- @LMGreenteam who cleared english ivy;

Thanks to everyone, this area on west side of Jericho Park that used to be infested with blackberry, knotweed & ivy is now a great example of what stewardship groups can achieve in a short time.\
