This year, JSG Society received a \$200 MEC gift card through the Grassroots Product Donation Program.  The funds helped to purchase 4 new folding wood saws for the volunteers to use while we work in the park and we rewarded some of the key-member volunteers to thank them for consistently contributing to our work in Jericho Park.

Thank-you [MEC](https://www.mec.ca/en/explore/grassroots-product-donations).
