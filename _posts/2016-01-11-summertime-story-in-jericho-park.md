Today, John told us of talking to a homeless gentleman during the summer drought who spent time and effort to bring water from public washrooms to keep some of our thirsty new plants alive.  Whoever you are … JSG and the Park thank you!  We know that Jericho is loved and appreciated by many many people.  Regularly, walkers & joggers on workparty mornings will stop to thank us for what we do.   That thanks goes to many others behind the scenes such as @ParksBoard staff, @EvergreenBC, @ISCMV, @LMGreenteam and one homeless man.

… a story worth sharing:)
