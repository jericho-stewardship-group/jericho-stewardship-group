There has been lots of noticeable changes in Jericho Park since last year when JSG created an ecological management plan for natural areas in Jericho in order to guide future work by us and Parks Board.  This has included PB using mowers to clear significant areas previously overgrown with Himalayan Blackberry and Japanese Knotweed.  Some cleared areas will be replanted with native trees while others will expand open meadow areas.
