## Conserving Jericho Beach Park for Future Generations

The Jericho Stewardship Group is a registered non-profit organization comprising residents, students, naturalists, and more. We work with the Vancouver Park Board to restore and encourage the natural habitat of Jericho Beach Park.

Our main activities include: invasive shrub removal and planting native species.

## Stewardship Events

Join us for one of our stewardship sessions to help us restore areas of the park that are overrun with invasive species. Our events are typically on the second Sunday of every month (weather and holidays permitting).
