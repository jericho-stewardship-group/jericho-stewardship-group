This report presents a comparative analysis of bird species observed in Jericho Park during 2006 – 2007 and 2020 – 2021. By examining changes in bird diversity and species composition over time, this study aims to shed light on the potential effects of environmental factors on avian populations within the park. Thank you to June Ryder and Mike Church for their hard work to create this report for the Jericho Stewardship Group.\
